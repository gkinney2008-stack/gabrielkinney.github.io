About

Building things that matter.

Hi, I'm Gabriel. I'm a mechanical engineering student at NC State University (and proud to be a Park and Stamps Scholar). I like figuring out how things work and, more importantly, how to make them work better for everyday people.

Whether I'm designing a new circuit board, writing firmware, or turning a concept into a 3D-printed prototype, I focus on building things that are accessible and sustainable. Over the years, I've had the chance to lead student engineering teams, publish research on advanced materials, and even start my own small business.

I just love bringing good ideas to life, especially when they can make a positive impact on the community.
